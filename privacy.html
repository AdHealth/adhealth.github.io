Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy describes how AdHealth (“we”) handles data when you authorize it to access Google Ads.

What we access

With your permission (Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords), AdHealth reads Google Ads account data: account and campaign settings, statuses, budgets, performance metrics and conversion tracking status.

How we use it

The data is used only to monitor account health and to prepare reports, alerts and recommendations for the account owner. Access is read-only: AdHealth never creates, changes or deletes anything in Google Ads.

Storage and sharing

  • Data is stored on servers located in the European Union and protected by access controls.
  • We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.
  • Data is shared only with service providers needed to run AdHealth (hosting, database, AI analysis), under their data-protection terms, and never made public.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AdHealth’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account (myaccount.google.com/permissions). On request we delete stored data for your accounts.
